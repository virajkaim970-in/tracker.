The app isn't on the Play Store yet — that's why Android will warn you when installing. Don't worry, it's safe.

1
Tap "Download APK"

Above. The download starts immediately — no sign-up, no waiting, no redirects.

2

3
Allow "Install unknown apps"

Android will say "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source." Tap Settings → enable the toggle → go back. This is normal for any app not on the Play Store.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the app really free?

Yes — you get a 3-day free trial with the entire app unlocked. After the trial, you can unlock it permanently for a one-time ₹30. No subscription, no hidden fees.

Which exams does it support?

Four: JEE (Physics, Chemistry, Maths), NEET (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Class 10 (all subjects), and Class 12 (with PCM, PCB, Humanities, and Commerce streams). You can switch exams anytime from Settings — your progress is saved separately for each.

Do I need internet to use it?

No. Once installed, the app works 100% offline. Your study data is stored locally on your phone and never uploaded anywhere.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage. Nothing is sent to any server. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

Will I lose my progress if I update the app?

No. Updates preserve all your existing data. That said, it's always smart to use the built-in Export button to back up your progress before a major update.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 7.0 (Nougat) and newer. This covers 98% of all Android phones in use today.

Why does Android show a warning when installing?

Any APK not distributed through the Play Store triggers this warning — it's Android being cautious. The app is safe, built from source, and shared publicly on GitHub.

Can I use it on multiple devices?

Each phone gets its own separate data (no sync between devices). For a single-device user, this is ideal — your data stays private. To move data between phones, use Export on the old phone and Import on the new one.

How do I unlock after the 3-day trial?

After 3 days, the app shows your unique Device ID. Show it to Viraj in school along with ₹30, and he'll give you a personalized unlock code. Enter the code once — the app is unlocked forever on that device.

Something's not working — who do I contact?

Reach out to Viraj directly on WhatsApp or in school. Bug reports and feature ideas are always welcome — this app was built for you.
