How to Install

  1. Tap the Download APK button above
  2. If Chrome warns "This type of file can harm your device" → tap Download anyway
  3. Open your phone's Files or Downloads app
  4. Tap JEE-Tracker.apk
  5. Android says "unknown apps" → tap Settings
  6. Enable "Allow from this source"
  7. Tap back → Install
  8. Done! Open JEE Tracker from your app drawer

FAQ

Is it safe? Yes. It's a WebView wrapper of this website. No trackers, no analytics, no data collection. Does it work offline? Yes. All data is stored locally on your phone.
